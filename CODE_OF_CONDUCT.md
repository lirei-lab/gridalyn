# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy toward other participants

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scientific conduct

This project exists to produce citable results, so a few norms are specific to
it:

- Report findings honestly, including those that weaken the case you were hoping
  to make. Several results in this repository are documented precisely because
  they were negative.
- Back claims about physical realism with a comparison against data, and state
  the population and conditions the comparison used.
- When you correct an earlier result, say what changed and why, rather than
  quietly replacing it.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **lirei.info@uqtr.ca**. Reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will
be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this
Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for
behaviour they deem inappropriate.
