    gridalyn flexibility and interaction vocabulary

    Curtailment contracts and the flexibility-market entities (aggregators, portfolios, providers, offers, constraint zones); the agents, parties, roles and conversations of the interaction protocols, with demand-response programs and events; and the predicates of the network-impact surrogate graph.

    • Namespace: https://w3id.org/gridalyn/ontology/flexint# (prefix flexint:)
    • Turtle: flexint.ttl
    • Generated from the declarations in gridalyn/twin/semantic/ by tools/export_ontology.py, and held to them by tests/test_ontology_documents.py; edit the declarations, not this page.

    Properties

    Domain and range are published as schema:domainIncludes and schema:rangeIncludes, not rdfs:domain/rdfs:range. They say which classes a property is meant to connect, and entail nothing: a relationship here may start at several unrelated classes, and rdfs:domain would assert that every subject belongs to all of them. gridalyn runs no reasoner and draws no inference from these statements. What holds the graph to them is validation: each relationship's predicate, domain, range and cardinality are checked against the profile's axioms when the graph is built (see Semantic Graph).
